ECHO / 2006

An investigation on an old phone

What was left unsaid?

You find a forgotten phone while studying in the library. Its messages point to a place called Echo and a friendship that ended without an explanation.

How to play

Explore the phone and collect clues. Answer the questions on the right. Some folders have limited password attempts. There is credit for one outgoing call.
